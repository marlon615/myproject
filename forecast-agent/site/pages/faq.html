The product

What does Draxly do?

Draxly forecasts the next 3 months of sales for every product at every store or warehouse, rolled up by region and in total. With your stock levels, it suggests what to reorder and sends low-stock alerts. It's built for US food and drink distributors and wholesalers.

Who is it for?

Food and drink distributors and wholesalers with roughly $20M to $500M in annual sales: companies with many products and locations, but usually no dedicated forecasting team.

How does the forecasting work?

For each product, Draxly tries several proven forecasting methods (recent average, trend, smoothing with a damped trend, and seasonal patterns once there are 2 or more years of history). It keeps the one that best predicted that product's own recent months. More on how it works.

How accurate is it?

It depends on your products and your history, so we don't quote a general number. Instead, Draxly measures it on your own data: it hides your last 3 months, predicts them, and shows predicted against actual by product, store and region. The free test demo lets you check it yourself against months you keep back.

How much history do I need?

At least 6 months to forecast and 9 months for the accuracy test. 12 to 24 months works best, and 2 or more years lets Draxly use seasonal patterns.

How many products can it handle?

Up to 50,000 product and location combinations per data set. We've tested the open demo and the full app with 50,000 products.

Does it forecast dollars or units?

Either or both. Reorder suggestions use units.

Do we need to install anything?

No. Draxly runs in the browser on desktop and phone.

Data and integrations

Which systems does Draxly connect to?

Oracle NetSuite, SAP S/4HANA, Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central, QuickBooks Online, Shopify and Square, with nightly syncs. Any other system can use the API, the HTTPS file drop, or a nightly file at a web address. You can also upload CSV or Excel files. See integrations.

How often is data refreshed?

With a connection, every night at about 2 a.m. in your time zone. The accuracy test and forecasts then update on their own.

Do you offer SFTP?

No. Draxly runs on Cloudflare, which doesn't accept SFTP connections. Most schedulers that drop files over SFTP can also send them to the HTTPS file drop.

Does Draxly change anything in our ERP?

No. Connections only read sales and stock. We recommend giving them read-only access.

Security and privacy

How is our data protected?

Each company's data is kept in its own separate storage. Sales figures, names, forecasts, stock levels and credentials are encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM. Sign-in supports Microsoft and Google single sign-on, two-factor codes and roles, and every important action is logged. Read the security overview.

Are you SOC 2 certified?

Not currently. We don't hold SOC 2 or ISO 27001 certification. We're happy to complete your security questionnaire.

Do you sell or share our data?

No. Your data is used only to provide Draxly to you. It isn't sold, shared for advertising, or used to serve other customers.

What happens to our data if we leave?

When an account is closed, all of that company's stored data is deleted. You can download your forecasts as CSV at any time before then.
