How the test works

Export 24 months, upload 21

Export your monthly sales by product from your system. Upload the first 21 months as a CSV or Excel file, and keep the last 3 real months to yourself.

Get the next 3 months

Draxly forecasts those 3 months for every product and in total, with a chart, a table by product and a CSV download.

Compare with what happened

Open the CSV next to the 3 months you kept back. The download has empty "Your actual" columns ready for your real numbers.

Private by design

Your file stays on your computer

  • Your spreadsheet is read in your own browser. Only monthly totals per product are sent, in small batches, to be forecast and sent straight back.
  • Nothing you upload is stored, and nothing stays after you close the page. Other visitors never see your data.
  • There's no sample data: the demo starts empty and shows only what you upload.
  • For each run we keep only the time, approximate city and country, device type, linking page, and the number of products and months.
