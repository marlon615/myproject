The agreement

Contract terms in plain English

These are the main points. The Terms of Service and your signed order form are the full agreement.

Price

$30,000 per company per year. One price for the whole company, with unlimited users.

Term

A one-year agreement, starting on the date in your order form.

Paying upfront

One invoice for $30,000 at the start of each agreement year.

Paying monthly

$2,500 a month, invoiced on the same day each month for the 12 months of the agreement year.

Invoices

Payable by the due date shown on each invoice, 15 days after it's issued. Payment details are on each invoice. Invoices and their PDFs are always available on your billing page.

Renewal

The agreement renews automatically each year unless cancelled before the renewal date.

Leaving early

You can cancel at any time. If you cancel before the end of an agreement year, the rest of that year is still owed, and the agreement doesn't renew.

Late payment

We send reminders 1, 7 and 14 days after the due date, then every 14 days. If an invoice stays unpaid, access may be paused until it's paid.

Your data

Your data stays yours. When an account is closed, all of that company's stored data is deleted.
