Data Processing Addendum

Last updated: November 17, 2026

This Data Processing Addendum ("DPA") forms part of the Terms of Service between M. D. Williams Enterprise, trading as Draxly ("Processor", "we"), and the Customer ("Customer", "you"). If you'd like a countersigned copy, email draxlyforecast@gmail.com.

1. Scope and roles

This DPA applies to personal information contained in Customer Data that we process to provide Draxly ("Customer Personal Data"). The Customer is the business (controller) and decides why and how Customer Personal Data is processed. We are the service provider (processor) and process it only on the Customer's behalf.

2. Details of processing

  • Purpose: providing the Draxly service: storing data, producing forecasts, accuracy tests, reorder suggestions, reports and alerts, and supporting the Customer.
  • Duration: for the term of the agreement, and until deletion under section 9.
  • Data subjects: the Customer's users; and any individuals whose details appear in the data the Customer provides (for example, named customers or locations).
  • Types of data: users' names, emails, roles and sign-in records (including IP addresses); and business data the Customer chooses to provide, such as sales, product, location and stock data. Draxly is designed for aggregated business data and doesn't need sensitive personal information; the Customer agrees not to provide it.

3. Processing only on instructions

We process Customer Personal Data only on the Customer's documented instructions, which are the agreement and the Customer's use and configuration of the service. We won't sell or share it, retain, use or disclose it outside the direct business relationship with the Customer, or combine it with personal information from other sources except as the law allows for a service provider. We'll tell the Customer if we believe an instruction breaks the law, or if we can no longer meet our obligations under applicable privacy law.

4. Confidentiality

Anyone we authorize to process Customer Personal Data is bound by confidentiality obligations and accesses it only as needed to provide and support the service.

5. Security measures

We maintain appropriate technical and organizational measures, including:

  • separate storage for each customer's data, with the customer worked out from the signed-in session or API key on every request;
  • encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and application-level AES-256-GCM encryption at rest for sales data, names, forecasts, stock levels, credentials and two-factor secrets, with the key held in the hosting provider's secret store;
  • single sign-on, two-factor sign-in, role-based access, limits on password and code guessing, and hashed passwords, API keys, reset links and invites;
  • an activity log of sign-ins, changes, uploads, syncs and API activity, available to the Customer's admins.

The current measures are described on our security page. We may update them, but won't reduce the overall level of protection.

6. Sub-processors

The Customer authorizes us to use these sub-processors:

  • Cloudflare, Inc.: hosting, storage and secrets.
  • Resend: sending email (invites, reports, alerts, invoices), when email is switched on.
  • Microsoft and Google: single sign-on, only if the Customer uses it.
  • ntfy.sh: notifications to our own phone, which may contain a user's company name and email when they ask for a password reset.

We bind each sub-processor to data protection terms appropriate to its service and remain responsible for its performance. We'll give at least 30 days' notice of a new sub-processor by updating this page and emailing the Customer's admins. The Customer may object on reasonable data protection grounds; if we can't address the objection, the Customer may end the affected service and receive a refund of prepaid fees for the unused period.

7. Requests and assistance

The service lets the Customer's admins view, export and delete data and manage users. If we receive a request from an individual about Customer Personal Data, we'll pass it to the Customer and won't respond ourselves unless the Customer asks us to or the law requires it. We'll provide reasonable help with data protection assessments and regulator inquiries relating to the service.

8. Security incidents

If we become aware of a breach of security leading to unauthorized access to, or loss or disclosure of, Customer Personal Data, we'll notify the Customer without undue delay. We'll provide the information we have to help the Customer meet its own obligations, and take reasonable steps to contain and fix the incident.

9. Deletion and return

During the term, the Customer can download its forecasts and delete uploads at any time. When the account is closed, we delete all of the Customer's stored data: sales, forecasts, stock, connections, the activity log, users and API keys. Billing records that the law requires us to keep are retained only for that purpose.

10. Audits

On reasonable request, no more than once a year (or after a security incident), we'll answer the Customer's written security questionnaire and provide information needed to show compliance with this DPA. We don't currently hold SOC 2 or ISO 27001 certification.

11. US state privacy laws

Where the California Consumer Privacy Act (as amended by the CPRA) or similar US state privacy laws apply, we act as a "service provider" or "processor", and the commitments in section 3 apply. We certify that we understand and will comply with these restrictions.

12. General

This DPA lasts as long as we process Customer Personal Data. If it conflicts with the Terms of Service on data protection, this DPA wins. The limitations of liability in the Terms of Service apply to this DPA.
