Test Draxly on your own sales

No sign-up. Upload 21 months of sales, keep the 3 finished months after them to yourself, and see how close Draxly's prediction gets to the real numbers you kept.

Today's cut-off

Your file must end in the cut-off month or earlier. Draxly will predict the 3 months to after it, which have already finished, so you can compare them with your real sales.

To keep the test fair, the demo only predicts months that are over. It never forecasts the future; that's what the full product is for. The cut-off moves forward by one month at the start of each month.

How it works

  1. Export 24 months of sales, 21 months before the cut-off to your last full month, as a spreadsheet (CSV or Excel): one row per sale, day, week or month, with a date, a product and the sales amount (or quantity).
  2. Keep the last 3 months back (the 3 months to after the cut-off). Remove them from the file and put them aside; don't upload them.
  3. Upload the 21 months, 21 months before the cut-off to the cut-off month, below. Check what Draxly found, then press “Predict the 3 months after my file”.
  4. Compare. Draxly predicts the 3 months to after the cut-off, the 3 months you kept, for every product and in total. Download the CSV and put your real numbers next to it.

Private: your file is read here in your browser. Only monthly totals are sent to Draxly, to be forecast and sent straight back — they're never stored, and no one else can see them. When you close this page or press “Start over”, they're gone.
