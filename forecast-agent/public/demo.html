Test Draxly on your own sales

No sign-up. Upload 21 months of sales, keep your most recent 3 months to yourself, and see how close Draxly's forecast gets to the real numbers you kept.

How it works

  1. Export 24 months of sales as a spreadsheet (CSV or Excel), up to your most recent full month — one row per sale, day, week or month, with a date, a product and the sales amount (or quantity).
  2. Keep your last 3 months back. Remove them from the file and put them aside — don't upload them. What's left is the 21 months before.
  3. Upload those 21 months below. Check what Draxly found, then press “Forecast the next 3 months”.
  4. Compare. Draxly forecasts the next 3 months — the 3 months you kept — for every product and in total. Download the CSV and put your real numbers next to it.

Private: your file is read here in your browser. Only monthly totals are sent to Draxly, to be forecast and sent straight back — they're never stored, and no one else can see them. When you close this page or press “Start over”, they're gone.
