Agreement terms

  • Price: $30,000 US per company per year.
  • Payment: either the full year upfront (one invoice at the start of each agreement year), or $2,500 a month (an invoice on the same date each month). Invoices are due 15 days after they're issued.
  • Renewal: the agreement renews automatically for another year unless it's cancelled before the renewal date.
  • Leaving early: if you cancel during an agreement year, the rest of that year is still owed. On the monthly option, the remaining months are invoiced together when you cancel.
  • Late payments: we send a reminder 1, 7 and 14 days after an invoice's due date, then every 14 days.

Questions

How do we pay? By bank transfer (ACH or wire) against our invoice. The details are on every invoice.

Can we try it with our real data first? Yes — open the demo, no sign-up. Upload 21 months of your sales, keep your last 3 months back, and compare Draxly's forecast with the real numbers. Nothing you upload is stored.

Where do we see our invoices? On the Billing page in Draxly (for your company's admins), with a PDF of every invoice.

What happens to our data if we leave? You can download your forecasts at any time. When an account is closed, all of its data is deleted.
