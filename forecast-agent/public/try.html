Your one-time Draxly demo

Please read first: this link works only once

When you press “Run my forecast”, this link is used up. You can't upload a second file, change columns afterwards, or try again. Get everything right before you press go.

Step by step

  1. Get your sales file ready. One spreadsheet (CSV or Excel) with your past sales: one row per sale, day, week or month. At least 12 months, ideally 24–36, up to your most recent full month.
  2. Check its columns. It needs a date, a product and a sales amount (or quantity). Store/warehouse, region and quantity are optional. Up to 500 product × location combinations.
  3. Choose your file below. It's read here in your browser — nothing is sent until you press the button.
  4. Check what Draxly found: the months, products and stores. Change the column choices if anything looks wrong.
  5. Tick the box and press “Run my forecast”. Your accuracy test and 3-month forecast appear on this page.
  6. Save your results. Download the forecast (CSV). Results stay viewable for 72 hours, then this link locks for good and your sales data is deleted.

Your data is private: encrypted, used only for your demo, and deleted when the link locks — unless you become a customer.
