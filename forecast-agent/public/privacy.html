1. Who we are

Draxly is provided by M. D. Williams Enterprise, trading as Draxly ("we", "us"). This policy explains how we handle personal information on our website, in the open demo, and in the Draxly service. Contact: draxlyforecast@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

When you contact us or request access

The details you enter: name, work email, company, and optionally job title, annual sales range, number of locations, systems you use, and your message. We also record the date and the approximate city and country of your network (as provided by our hosting provider), and keep a one-way hash of your network address for up to an hour to limit repeated submissions.

When you use the open demo

Your spreadsheet is read in your browser. Only monthly totals per product are sent to us to be forecast in memory and sent straight back; they're not stored. For each run we record the time, approximate city and country, device type, the page that linked to the demo, and the file's size (number of products and months). We don't record IP addresses, file names, product names or sales figures for demo runs.

When you use the service

  • Account information: users' names, email addresses, roles, and sign-in settings (such as whether two-factor sign-in is on).
  • Activity log: sign-ins and changes, with the user, time and IP address, kept so your admins can review account activity.
  • Customer data: the sales, product, location and stock data your company provides, and the forecasts produced from it. See section 4.
  • Billing information: the company's name, agreement details and invoices.

Browsing the website

The website doesn't use analytics, advertising or tracking tools. Our hosting provider processes standard technical information, such as IP addresses, to deliver pages and protect the service.

3. How we use it

  • To provide, secure and support the service, and to send service emails such as invites, password resets, reports, alerts and invoices.
  • To reply to your messages and access requests.
  • To bill for the service and keep business records.
  • To understand, in aggregate, how the demo is used (for example, file sizes and which pages link to it).
  • To meet legal obligations and protect our rights.

We don't sell personal information, and we don't share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

4. Customer data

For data your company puts into Draxly, your company is in control and we process it on its behalf, under our Terms of Service and Data Processing Addendum. If you're an employee of one of our customers and have a request about that data, please contact your company first.

5. Who we share it with

We use these service providers, only for the purposes described:

  • Cloudflare: hosting, storage and secrets.
  • Resend: sending email, when email is switched on.
  • Microsoft and Google: single sign-on, if your company uses it.
  • ntfy.sh: notifications to our own phone. These contain the name, company and email from a contact form or access request, or the company name and email when someone asks for a password reset.
  • The business systems your company connects, which Draxly contacts on its behalf.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, to protect rights and safety, or as part of a merger or sale of our business, in which case this policy would continue to apply.

6. Cookies and local storage

We don't set advertising or analytics cookies. The signed-in app stores a session token in your browser's local storage so you stay signed in; signing out removes it.

7. How long we keep it

  • Contact and access requests: as long as needed to handle the request and any resulting relationship; you can ask us to delete them at any time.
  • Customer data and accounts: for as long as the account is open. When an account is closed, all of that company's stored data is deleted.
  • Billing records: as long as tax and accounting laws require.
  • Demo data: the files and sales figures are never stored; the run details above are kept to understand demo use.

8. Security

We protect information with encryption in transit and at rest, separate storage for each company, strict access controls and an activity log. Read the details on our security page. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and will notify affected customers of a breach as the law requires.

9. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live (for example, California under the CCPA/CPRA, or other US states with privacy laws), you may have the right to know what personal information we hold about you, to get a copy, to correct it, and to have it deleted, and not to be treated differently for using these rights. To make a request, email draxlyforecast@gmail.com. We'll verify the request and respond within the time the law requires. You can use an authorized agent where the law allows.

10. Children

Draxly is a business service and isn't meant for children. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 16.

11. Changes to this policy

We'll update this page when our practices change and change the "Last updated" date. For significant changes, we'll notify customers by email.
