        Reorder suggestions

        Reorder point = expected daily demand × lead time + safety stock (from the forecast's own error, at your service level). When stock on hand + on order is at or below it, the suggestion orders up to the reorder point plus your review period's demand, rounded to the case pack.

        Stock levels

        Upload a CSV or Excel file with product, store/warehouse (optional) and on hand; optionally on order, lead time (days), case pack, minimum order and unit cost. Product and store names must match your sales data. Connected Shopify, Square and QuickBooks accounts send stock levels every night.

        Connected systems

        Sales (and stock where the system has it) are pulled in automatically every night at about 2am your time; each night re-reads the last two months to catch late changes.
