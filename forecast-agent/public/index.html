Why Draxly

Stock decisions based on what your own customers buy

Too much stock ties up cash and spoils. Too little loses orders. Draxly gives your buyers a clear 3-month view, built and tested on your own numbers.

Forecasts at every level

The next 3 months for each product, at each store or warehouse, rolled up by region and in total. The parts always add up to the whole.

Accuracy you can check

Draxly hides your last 3 months, predicts them, and shows predicted against actual by product and location before you rely on it.

Reorder suggestions

With your stock on hand, lead times and case packs, Draxly suggests what to order and how much, and emails low-stock alerts.

Works with your systems

Nightly connections to NetSuite, SAP S/4HANA, Dynamics 365 Business Central, QuickBooks Online, Shopify and Square, plus an API and HTTPS file drop.

Weekly reports

A weekly forecast report by email for each team member who switches it on, so the numbers reach the people who place orders.

Enterprise security

Single sign-on with Microsoft and Google, two-factor sign-in, roles, a full activity log, and AES-256 encryption of your sales data at rest.
