About

Forecasting built for the people who buy the stock

Draxly is made by M. D. Williams Enterprise for US food and drink distributors and wholesalers.

Why Draxly exists

Distributors live between two costly mistakes: too much stock, which ties up cash and spoils, and too little, which loses orders and customers. Purchasing is often planned in spreadsheets, from experience and last year's numbers.

Draxly gives those teams the kind of planning that larger companies get from dedicated forecasting staff: a clear, tested 3-month forecast for every product and location, and reorder suggestions built on it.
