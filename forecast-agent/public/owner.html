One-time demo links

When a company says yes, send them a link that runs one full forecast on their own sales file. After that run their results stay viewable for 72 hours, then the link locks for good and their data is deleted (unless you mark them as a customer). Unused links lock after 30 days. You see each link's status, never their data.

Email (for invites, weekly reports and low-stock alerts)

Uses Resend (free tier: 3,000 emails a month). Create an account at resend.com, verify your domain there, create an API key, and paste it with the From address (e.g. Draxly <billing@yourdomain.com>). Without a verified domain, Resend only delivers to your own address.
