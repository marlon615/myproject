Frequently asked questions

Straight answers. If yours isn’t here, ask us and we’ll reply by email.

The app

What is Marlon?

Marlon is delivery invoicing and route sales software that runs on a phone. Reps write invoices, the store signs on the screen, and the invoice is shared by WhatsApp, PDF, email, QR code or a printed receipt. It works with no internet, and for teams the boss sees every invoice in one dashboard.

Does it really work with no internet?

Yes. The first time a personal link is opened, the phone needs internet. After that, writing invoices, signing, sharing a PDF or picture, printing and the owner’s dashboard all work offline. Team invoices wait on the phone and go to the boss as soon as there’s a connection.

Which phones does it work on?

Modern Android phones with Chrome and iPhones with Safari. Marlon installs from the browser to the home screen, so there’s no app store. Bluetooth receipt printing needs an Android phone with Chrome.

Do we need to buy any hardware?

No. Marlon runs on the phones your reps already have. A Bluetooth receipt printer (58 mm or 80 mm, ESC/POS) is optional.

What kinds of businesses is it for?

Any business that delivers or sells on a route: distributors, wholesalers, bakeries, farms, drinks and snacks suppliers, and online sellers whose buyers live in another town. It suits one person with one phone just as well as a company with a hundred reps.

Can we use our own products and prices?

Yes. Marlon starts blank. You type your products, each with a usual price and how it’s sold (each, by the dozen, by the bag or by the pound). Any price can be changed on any invoice.

How does voice entry work?

Tap “Speak the invoice” and say the store, the address and the products. Marlon matches what you say to your own product list by sound and shows a review screen, highlighting anything unclear, before anything goes on the invoice. Voice entry is in English.

How does the store get its copy?

However it prefers: a QR code it scans with its own phone (a text-only QR code works with no internet), a PDF or picture sent by WhatsApp or email, or a printed receipt.

Teams and the boss

How does the boss see the reps’ invoices?

Every invoice is saved on the rep’s phone first. Whenever there’s internet, it’s sent to the boss dashboard automatically, within about 10 seconds. The boss sees every invoice from every rep, with totals for today, this week and this month.

What stops a rep sharing their link with someone else?

Each rep link locks to the first phone that opens it. Any other phone that opens the same link is blocked, so passing it on does nothing.

What happens when a rep leaves?

The boss taps “Switch off” for that rep. Their phone stops working the next time it’s online, their invoices stay under their name, and the place is freed. “Give to new rep” hands the place to someone new, and the old phone can never come back on it.

Can the boss change a rep’s invoice?

No. The boss can mark an invoice paid and manage reps, but can’t write or edit invoices. Each invoice stays exactly as the rep made it and the store signed it.

How many bosses can use the boss dashboard?

The boss dashboard works on up to two boss phones, with the boss’s own passcode.

Pricing and payment

How much does Marlon cost?

$79 one-time per rep, covering the first year. A solo business counts as one rep. Minor updates are always free. Teams get an automatic discount: 10% from 5 reps, 15% from 10, 20% from 25, 25% from 50 and 30% from 100. See Pricing.

How much is Marlon Pro?

$25 per rep per month, with the same team discounts. It’s optional and billed monthly in advance. You can cancel any time; Pro stays on until the end of the month you’ve paid for.

Data and security

Where are our invoices stored?

On the phone, in the browser’s own storage. A solo business’s invoices never leave its phone unless it shares them. For a team, invoices are also kept on our server (hosted by Cloudflare) so the boss dashboard can show them. See Security and privacy.

Do you sell or look at our data?

No. We don’t sell, rent or share your data, and there are no ads. The anonymous usage counts a phone sends never include names, customers, products, prices or amounts, and can be turned off in Settings.

What if a phone is lost or the browser is cleared?

Data on that phone is lost unless you have a backup or, for a team, it was already sent to the boss. Make backups from the owner’s dashboard. The boss can switch off a lost phone so it can’t be used for your company.

Does Marlon have security certifications?

No, and we won’t claim otherwise. Our security page explains exactly how Marlon protects phones and data, and we’re happy to answer your company’s security questionnaire in writing.
