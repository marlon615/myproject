Every feature, in plain words

Everything below is in Marlon today. Features marked Pro come with the Marlon Pro add-on.

Invoices

The heart of Marlon: fast, clear delivery invoices.

  • Your own products and prices, typed once at setup and changed any time.
  • Sold each, by the dozen, by the bag or by the pound, shown on every line.
  • Tick what you delivered: the usual price fills in and can be changed on any invoice.
  • Totals that add themselves up, line by line and for the whole invoice.
  • Store, address and date on every invoice, with numbered invoices (No. 0001, 0002…).
  • Add a product on the spot from the invoice screen.
  • Your business name, address and phone at the top of every copy.
  • “Thank you, [store]” at the bottom of every customer copy.
  • Saved as you type, so nothing is lost if the phone locks or the app closes.
  • Delete an invoice made by mistake, from the history.

Voice entry

Say the invoice instead of typing it.

  • Say it naturally: “two dozen mango juice, fifty pounds sweet potato at one twenty”.
  • Store and address picked out of what you say, from clear address phrases only.
  • Matched by sound against your own product list, so local product names work.
  • A review screen shows what the phone heard and highlights anything unclear.
  • One-tap suggestions when a word could be more than one product.
  • Usual prices fill in where no price was said, and no spoken line is ever dropped.
  • English. Uses the phone’s own speech recognition, on the phone itself where the phone supports it, with the keyboard’s dictation as a fallback.

Signatures

Proof of delivery the store gives with a finger.

  • Sign on the rep’s phone, right on the invoice.
  • “Thank you” and the store’s name written in ink as the customer signs.
  • Done shows a checkmark with a gentle tap, then the invoice is ready to share.
  • Changing a signed invoice asks first, so a signature always matches what was signed.
  • Send for signature: a secure link for a buyer in another town, signed on their own phone with no app or login.
  • Locked when signed: the buyer can only type their name and sign; the signed copy comes back to the seller and can’t be changed.
  • Sent, Opened, Signed and Expired statuses. Unsigned links expire after 7 days.
  • Alerts when they sign, even with Marlon closed (on iPhone: Marlon added to the Home Screen, iOS 16.4 or later).

Sharing and printing

The store’s copy, the way the store wants it.

  • PDF invoice on a clean letter-size page, shared by WhatsApp, email or any app on the phone.
  • Picture of the receipt, handy for WhatsApp.
  • QR code: the store scans it with its own phone camera to see the invoice.
  • Text-only QR code for a store phone with no internet.
  • Private customer page: the invoice travels inside the QR link itself and is never sent to a server.
  • Bluetooth receipt printers, 58 mm and 80 mm (ESC/POS), from Android phones with Chrome.
  • Save PDF or print from the customer’s page too.

Payments

Know who owes what, and when.

  • Cash, 7 days or 14 days, chosen after signing on a page only the owner sees.
  • Every invoice keeps its own due date.
  • Unpaid invoices turn red from their due day until “Payment received”, never by themselves.
  • Payment never shows on the customer’s copies or the signing screen.
  • Teams: the boss marks invoices paid, and the rep’s phone shows it too.

Owner dashboard

On the phone, locked with a PIN.

  • Totals for today, this week, this month and all time.
  • Unpaid invoices with their due dates.
  • History of every invoice, with its status.
  • “Your business in numbers”: best customer, busiest day, monthly totals, top stores and products sold.
  • Settings: business details, products and prices, printer, backup and restore.
  • PIN lock that makes you wait longer after each wrong try.
  • Backup file you can keep safe and restore on a phone.
  • Anonymous counts on or off: you choose whether the phone sends anonymous usage counts.

Teams and the boss

One company, many reps, one view.

  • Boss dashboard with its own passcode, on up to two boss phones.
  • Every invoice from every rep: rep, customer, products, total, paid or not paid.
  • Totals per rep and for the company: today, this week (Monday to Sunday), this month.
  • Automatic sending: new and changed invoices reach the boss within about 10 seconds when online.
  • No signal? Invoices wait on the rep’s phone and go as soon as there’s a connection.
  • Mark paid, which the rep’s phone can never undo.
  • The boss can’t write or edit invoices, so every invoice stays as the store signed it.
  • Last data kept on the boss phone when it’s offline.

Phones and control

Only the phones you paid for, and only for as long as you want.

  • A personal link for each rep, locked to the first phone that opens it.
  • Passing a link on does nothing: any other phone is blocked.
  • Add rep by name, never beyond the places you paid for.
  • Switch off a rep: their phone stops working and their invoices stay under their name.
  • Give to new rep: hand a place on; the old phone can never come back on it.
  • Phones check in whenever they’re online, so a switched-off phone stops at its next check.

Offline and installing

Built for routes where the signal comes and goes.

  • Works with no internet after the first open with a connection.
  • Installs from the browser to the home screen, on Android and iPhone. No app store.
  • Data kept on the phone in the browser’s own storage.
  • Updates install by themselves.
  • No special hardware: any modern phone. A Bluetooth printer is optional.
  • Starts blank: each business sets up its own name, products and prices.

Marlon Pro Pro

The add-on for companies that want more. More about Pro →

  • My Products: your company’s own products with size, price and up to 3 camera photos; the boss’s list reaches every rep phone.
  • Shelf photo: which of your products are on a shelf, and how many, from one photo; anything else shows as “Unknown product”.
  • Shelf Order: the order fills itself from the shelf count (target − on shelf), signed by the store person, numbered ORD-0001.
  • Orders to the boss: Pending, Delivered or Cancelled, with who ordered; a delivered order becomes an invoice in one tap.
  • Accountant summary by quarter and for the year, as a CSV download or a PDF.
  • Returns and credit notes, numbered and signed by the store.
  • Credit taken off the store’s next invoice automatically.
  • Returns reports by store, by product (expired or damaged) and by rep.
  • Works offline too: returns, orders and shelf photos wait on the phone until it’s online.
Good to know

What Marlon needs

A modern phone and browser

Android with Chrome, or iPhone with Safari. Marlon installs from the browser to the home screen.

Internet once, at the start

Opening the personal link the first time needs a connection. After that, invoicing works with no internet.

Printing needs Android

Bluetooth receipt printing uses Web Bluetooth, which works in Chrome on Android. On iPhone, share the PDF or picture instead.
