How Marlon works, from the boss to the reps to the stores

Five short stages, from your first message to a signed invoice in a store’s hands. No hardware to buy, no app store, and nothing to install on a computer.

1. Getting Marlon

You and the Marlon Team, by email

  1. Tell us about your business

    Use the contact form or email marlonapp.sales@gmail.com. Tell us whether you work alone or with reps, and how many reps you have.

  2. Get your exact price and the terms

    We reply with your price, with any team discount for your number of reps, and the terms for your company.

  3. Sign the terms and get your official invoice

    Once the terms are signed, you receive the official invoice. The payment details are on that invoice and nowhere else. See How to pay.

  4. Receive your links

    When payment is received, your company is set up. A solo business gets one personal link. A team gets one boss link and one link for each rep place you paid for.

2. The boss

Sets up the company dashboard

  1. Open the boss link and set a passcode

    The first visit sets the boss’s own passcode. The boss dashboard works on up to two boss phones. After five wrong tries, it waits 15 minutes.

  2. Name each rep and send their link

    “Add rep” names one of the rep places you paid for and shows that rep’s personal link, ready to copy or share by WhatsApp. It can never go beyond the number of places you paid for.

  3. Stay in control as the team changes

    A rep leaves? “Switch off” stops their phone the next time it’s online, keeps their invoices under their name and frees the place. “Give to new rep” hands the place on, and the old phone can never come back on it.

3. Each rep

One link, one phone, ready for the road

  1. Open the personal link once, with internet

    The link locks to the first phone that opens it. Anyone else who opens the same link is blocked and shown who to contact. On iPhone, Marlon first asks to be added to the Home Screen, so it works like an app.

  2. Set up in a few minutes

    The company name is already filled in and locked. The rep types their own name, then the products they sell, each with a usual price and how it’s sold: each, by the dozen, by the bag or by the pound.

  3. Work anywhere

    From then on Marlon works with no internet. Every invoice is saved on the phone first and goes to the boss automatically whenever there’s a connection.

4. At every store

The invoice, the signature, the copy

  1. Write the invoice

    Type or say the store and address, tick the products delivered and set the quantities. Usual prices fill in, any price can be changed, and the totals add themselves up.

  2. The store signs on the phone

    The store signs with a finger. “Thank you” and the store’s name are written in ink as they sign, then Done shows a checkmark with a gentle tap.

  3. The store gets its copy

    Show a QR code the store scans with its own phone (a text-only one works with no internet), share a PDF or a picture by WhatsApp or email, or print a receipt on a 58 or 80 mm Bluetooth printer.

  4. Record how it’s being paid

    Cash, 7 days or 14 days, on a page only the owner sees. Unpaid invoices show their due date and turn red from the due day until the payment is received.

Buyer in another town? Send for signature makes a secure link the buyer opens on their own phone, with no app and no login. They read the whole invoice, sign and download their copy. The signed copy comes back to the rep’s phone and is locked for good.

5. Every day after

The boss sees the whole company

Live totals

Today, this week (Monday to Sunday) and this month, per rep and for the whole company.

Mark paid

The boss ticks a rep’s unpaid invoice as paid. The rep’s phone shows it within about a minute when online, and the rep can’t undo it.

Nothing rewritten

The boss sees every invoice but can’t write or edit them. Each invoice stays exactly as the rep made it and the store signed it.

Working alone?

A solo business works the same way, without the boss dashboard

One personal link, one phone. The owner’s dashboard on the phone, locked with a PIN, shows totals, unpaid invoices with due dates, every invoice and “Your business in numbers”. A solo business’s invoices never leave its phone.
