How to pay for Marlon

Payment details are given only on your official invoice, after the terms are signed. They are never shown on this website or sent in any other way.

  1. Ask for your price

    Send us a message through the contact form or email marlonapp.sales@gmail.com with your company name and number of reps.

  2. Receive your price and terms in writing

    We confirm your exact price by email: Marlon at $79 per rep, one-time, with any team discount, and Marlon Pro at $25 per rep a month if you want it.

  3. Sign the terms

    You review and sign the terms for your company. Nothing is paid before this step.

  4. Receive your official invoice

    Your official invoice from M. D. Williams Enterprise shows the amount due and the payment details. Pay using only the details on that invoice.

  5. Get your links

    Once your payment is received, your company is set up and we email your personal links: one for a solo business, or a boss link and one link for each rep.

Protect yourself from fake payment requests

  • We never publish bank details on this website, in ads or on social media.
  • We send payment details only on your official invoice, after the terms are signed.
  • We will never ask you to pay into a different account by phone, text or chat.
  • If anything looks different from your invoice, don’t pay. Email marlonapp.sales@gmail.com first and we’ll confirm.

Marlon Pro payments

  • Marlon Pro is billed monthly, in advance, for every rep place your company has.
  • Your monthly price and payment date are confirmed in a payment notice when Pro is switched on.
  • If a monthly payment isn’t made, Pro may be switched off until it is. Regular Marlon keeps working.
  • You’ll be told at least 30 days before any change to the Pro price.

All prices are in US dollars. Marlon doesn’t take card payments inside the app or on this website. See the terms for the full Marlon Pro billing rules.
