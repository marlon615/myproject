More from every route, every shelf and every return

Shelf photo

One photo of the shelf. Your products, counted.

The rep photographs a store’s shelf. Marlon shows which of your company’s products are on it and how many, so the rep knows what to restock before writing the invoice.

  • Only your products. Names that aren’t on your company’s product list are left out.
  • Works on the road. With no signal, photos wait on the phone and are checked once it’s online.
  • Light on data. The photo is shrunk on the phone to about 200 KB before it’s sent.
  • Up to 100 shelf photos a day for each company.

Counts are made by AI (Google’s Gemini service) and can be wrong. Check the shelf before relying on them. Checks can be unavailable on some days if the service’s free daily limit is reached.

Shelf Order

From the shelf count to a signed order, then an invoice

After a shelf photo, the rep taps “Make an order”. The order fills itself from what’s on the shelf. Set a target for each product and Marlon works out how many to order.

  • Target minus shelf. Want 20 of each on the shelf and 6 are there? Marlon orders 14. Set one target for everything, or a different one per product.
  • The rep has the last word. Any amount can be changed, and products added or left out, before saving.
  • Signed by the store. The person placing the order types their name and signs on the phone. The name, signature, store, date and time are saved with the order.
  • Numbered and tracked. ORD-0001, ORD-0002… each shows as Pending, Delivered or Cancelled.
  • Straight to the boss. When the rep taps Done, the order goes to the boss dashboard (or the owner’s dashboard for a solo business), or waits offline and sends itself when there’s signal.
  • Proof at delivery. The driver opens the order on the phone and shows who ordered it, with their name and signature, even if that person isn’t there. Share it as a PDF for a driver on another phone.
  • Invoice in one tap. When it’s delivered, “Delivered: make the invoice” turns the order into an invoice with the same products, amounts and usual prices.

Accountant summary

Quarter and year-end figures, ready for your accountant

Marlon puts together your sales, returns and payments by quarter (January to March, and so on) and for the year ending 31 December, straight from the invoices and credit notes in Marlon.

  • For each quarter and the year: invoices, gross sales, returns, net sales, received, and not yet paid.
  • Download for your accountant (CSV): the summary plus every invoice and credit-note line, with the rep.
  • Print or save as PDF in one tap.
  • In the boss dashboard for a team, or in the owner’s dashboard for a solo business.

The accountant summary isn’t tax or accounting advice. Your accountant still checks it before you file your taxes.

Returns and credit notes

Returns handled at the store, credited on the next invoice

When a store hands back expired or damaged goods, the rep records the return on the phone, even offline. The store signs, and Marlon creates a numbered credit note.

  • Each returned product with its quantity and reason: expired or damaged.
  • Numbered credit notes (CN-0001, CN-0002…) signed by the store and shared as a PDF.
  • Taken off automatically: the credit comes off that store’s next invoice, oldest credit first, never more than the invoice. Anything left over carries to the next one.
  • Shown clearly on the invoice, receipt, PDF and customer page: subtotal, credit note, total.
  • Paid or signed invoices keep their credit, and deleting an invoice gives its credit back.
  • Returns reports by store, by product (expired or damaged) and, for teams, by rep. The boss sees which rep issued each credit note.
