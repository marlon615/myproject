Built for the road, not the office

Marlon is made by M. D. Williams Enterprise in Gutters, St. Catherine, Jamaica, for businesses whose sales happen at a store counter, from the back of a van, often where the signal drops.

Why Marlon exists

Delivery invoicing shouldn’t depend on a signal bar

Route sales still run on paper invoice books, carbon copies and end-of-day phone calls in many places. Software that could replace them usually expects a laptop, a constant connection or a long setup.

Marlon starts from the opposite end: the rep’s own phone, at the store, with or without internet. Everything a delivery invoice needs (the products, the prices, the signature, the store’s copy) happens right there. The boss gets the picture without chasing anyone.

It is built to work the same for one person with one van as for a company with a hundred reps.

What we stand for

The promises we build by

Offline first

If a feature can work without internet, it does. The phone is the place where the work happens.

Your data is yours

Invoices stay on the phone unless they need to reach your boss. We don’t sell data, show ads or add trackers.

Plain pricing

A one-time price per rep, a clear discount table and an optional monthly add-on. Your price is confirmed in writing before you pay.

Honest limits

We tell you what Marlon can’t do as clearly as what it can. No fake reviews, customers or certifications.

Help in writing

Every question gets a written answer by email, so you always have a record of what was agreed.

Always improving

Updates install on the phones by themselves, and minor updates are always free.
