What’s included

The details, up front

The first year is covered

Your one-time Marlon payment covers your first year of Marlon. What’s included after the first year is set out in your terms before you pay, so there are no surprises.

Minor updates are always free

Fixes and minor improvements are free, in the first year and after. Updates install on the phones by themselves.

Priced per rep

You pay for the number of rep places your company needs. For a team, the boss dashboard is included. A solo business counts as one rep.

Confirmed in writing

Your exact price, with any team discount and special deal, is on your official invoice after the terms are signed. See How to pay.

Pricing questions

Is there a monthly fee for Marlon?

No. Marlon is a one-time payment of $79 per rep, less any team discount. Only Marlon Pro, the optional add-on, is monthly.

Does the boss need to pay for a rep place?

No. For a team, the boss dashboard is included. You pay for the rep places your company needs.

Can we add reps later?

Yes. Email us about adding reps and we’ll confirm the price for the extra places. For Marlon Pro, places added during a month are billed from the start of the next month.

Do you take card payments on this website?

No. Nothing is paid on this website. The payment details come on your official invoice after the terms are signed.

Can I cancel Marlon Pro?

Yes, any time, by telling us. Pro stays on until the end of the month you’ve paid for, then switches off. Part-months aren’t refunded, and nothing is deleted.
