What Marlon does

Everything a delivery invoice needs, on the phone your rep already carries

No paper invoice books, no laptop in the van, no waiting for signal. Marlon installs from a personal link and works the same on the busiest street and the quietest country road.

Works offline

After the first open, Marlon works with no internet at all. Invoices, signatures, receipts and the owner dashboard all live on the phone. Team invoices go to the boss by themselves when there’s a connection.

Rep links locked to one phone

Every rep gets a personal link. It locks to the first phone that opens it, so passing it on does nothing. A rep leaves? Switch their phone off and hand the place to the next rep.

At the store

Write it, sign it, send it, before the rep leaves the store

Tick the products delivered, set the quantities, and Marlon fills in the usual prices and adds up the totals. The store signs on the screen and the rep taps Done.

  • Your products, your prices. Sold each, by the dozen, by the bag or by the pound. A price can be changed on any invoice.
  • “Thank you, Bay Market.” The store’s name is written in ink as they sign, and every copy ends with a thank-you line.
  • Payment terms that stay private. Cash, 7 days or 14 days, seen only by the owner and the boss, never on the customer’s copy.

For the boss

Every sale from every rep, without chasing anyone

Each invoice is saved on the rep’s phone first. Whenever there’s internet, it goes to the boss dashboard on its own, usually within seconds. With no signal it waits and sends itself later.

  • Totals that add themselves up: today, this week and this month, per rep and for the whole company.
  • Mark paid in one tap. It shows as paid on the rep’s phone too, and a rep can never undo it.
  • Add, switch off or replace reps without ever going over the number of places you paid for.

How it works

From the boss, to the reps, to the stores

  1. The boss

    Gets one boss link and one link per rep

    After the terms are signed and paid, your company is set up. The boss opens the boss link, sets a passcode and names each rep.

  2. Each rep

    Opens their link once

    Marlon installs on the rep’s home screen and locks to that phone. The rep types the products and prices once, and from then on it works offline.

  3. Every store

    Signs and gets its copy

    The store signs on the phone and gets its invoice by WhatsApp, PDF, email, QR code or a printed receipt. The boss sees the sale.
