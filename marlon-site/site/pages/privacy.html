Privacy policy

This policy explains what information Marlon and this website collect, why, and what you can do about it. We’ve written it to be read, not skimmed past.

In short: a business’s invoices, customers and products are kept on its own phone. For a team, invoices also go to the company’s boss dashboard. We collect only what we need to run Marlon and answer you. We don’t sell data, show ads or use tracking cookies.

Who we are

Marlon is provided by M. D. Williams Enterprise, Gutters, St. Catherine, Jamaica (“we”, “us”). For anything about this policy or your information, email {{email}}.

This website

  • No cookies, analytics or trackers. We don’t add any analytics, advertising or tracking tools to this website.
  • The contact form. When you send a message, we keep what you type: your name, company, email address, role, number of reps, topic and message. We use it only to reply to you and to handle your purchase.
  • Spam protection. To limit how many messages can be sent, we keep a one-way, salted hash of your IP address for up to 24 hours. We never store the IP address itself.
  • Hosting. The website is hosted by Cloudflare, which processes technical information such as IP addresses to deliver the pages and protect them from attack.

The Marlon app

Marlon keeps a business’s details, products, customers, invoices and signatures on the phone, in the browser’s own storage. For a solo business, this information stays on the phone. It is not sent to us, except in the cases described below or when the business shares an invoice itself.

To check that each phone is allowed to use Marlon, the app sends our server:

  • the personal link’s key and a random id made for the phone;
  • the type of phone and browser (from the browser’s user-agent);
  • the days the phone was first and last seen;
  • for a team, the rep’s or boss’s name as given in Marlon.

When you buy Marlon, we also keep the buyer’s name, the number of phones or rep places paid for, the amount paid, and the contact details you give us (such as a phone number and email address) so we can send payment notices and support you.

Teams and the boss dashboard

For a team, each invoice is saved on the rep’s phone and then sent to our server, so the company’s boss dashboard can show it. This includes the invoice number, date, customer (store and address), products, quantities, prices, totals, whether it was signed, and payment status (paid or not, and the due date). The signature picture itself is not sent. With Marlon Pro, the rep’s credit notes are sent the same way, also without the signature picture.

This information can be seen through your company’s passcode-locked boss dashboard. We don’t use it for any other purpose, and the console we use to manage buyers doesn’t show invoice contents.

Send for signature and alerts

  • When a seller sends an invoice for signature, a frozen copy is stored on our server, encrypted (AES-256-GCM). When the buyer signs, their typed name and signature are stored encrypted too. Only a hash of the secret link is stored.
  • If a seller turns on signing alerts, the phone’s push subscription is stored encrypted, and used only to tell that phone when an invoice is signed. Subscriptions that the push service reports as gone are deleted.

Marlon Pro shelf orders

A shelf order is saved on the rep’s phone with the store, the products and amounts, and the name and signature of the person who placed it, with the date and time. For a team with Marlon Pro, the order is also sent to our server so the company’s boss dashboard can show it. The orderer’s name and signature are stored encrypted (AES-256-GCM). The boss dashboard shows the name, and shows the signature only when the boss taps “Show signature”. For a solo business, orders stay on its own phone.

Marlon Pro shelf photo

When a rep takes a shelf photo with Marlon Pro, the photo (shrunk on the phone) and the company’s product names are sent through our server to Google’s Gemini service, which reads the photo. Our server doesn’t keep the photo after it has been read. The phone keeps a small copy in its own history. Google processes the photo under its own Gemini API terms. Please don’t photograph people or private documents.

Anonymous usage counts

After setup, each phone can send anonymous counts of how Marlon is used: a random id plus “what happened, which day” (for example, an invoice made or a receipt printed). These counts never include names, customers, products, prices or amounts. A business can turn them off at any time in Settings.

Who we share with

We don’t sell, rent or trade your information, and we don’t use it for advertising. We share it only with:

  • Cloudflare, which hosts Marlon and this website and stores the server-side data described above;
  • Google, only for shelf photos with Marlon Pro, as described above;
  • push services (such as Google, Apple, Mozilla or Microsoft), only to deliver signing alerts a seller turned on;
  • authorities, if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • Contact form messages: as long as we need them to answer you and keep a record of your purchase, or until you ask us to delete them.
  • Spam-protection hashes: up to 24 hours.
  • Team invoices, credit notes and shelf orders: while your company uses Marlon. Ask us to delete them when you stop.
  • Information on a phone stays on the phone until the business deletes it or the browser’s data is cleared. Marlon’s settings include a way to erase everything on the phone.

Security

Information travels over HTTPS. Sensitive server-side data is encrypted as described above, and dashboards are locked with passcodes or PINs. The security page explains the details and the limits.
