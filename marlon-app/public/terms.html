Marlon Pro Pro

Marlon Pro is an optional add-on to Marlon. It adds Returns and credit notes, Shelf photo, and the Accountant summary and returns reports in the boss dashboard (or, for a solo business, in its own dashboard). Everything in regular Marlon keeps working exactly as before, with or without Pro.

Price

$25 (US dollars) per rep, per month

  • A solo business (one business on its own phone, without reps) counts as one rep.
  • If you have a percentage discount on regular Marlon, the same percentage discount applies to Marlon Pro.
  • Your monthly Pro price, with any discount, is confirmed to you before Pro is switched on for your company.

Per-rep monthly billing

  • Pro is billed monthly, in advance, for every rep place your company has on Marlon: for a team, the number of rep places the company paid for (shown in the boss dashboard); for a solo business, one.
  • Rep places added during a month are billed from the start of the next month. Rep places removed stop being billed from the start of the next month.
  • Payment is made to the person who sold you Marlon, as agreed with them. Marlon doesn't take card payments inside the app.
  • If a monthly payment isn't made, Pro may be switched off until it is. Regular Marlon keeps working.
  • You'll be told at least 30 days before any change to the Pro price.

Cancellation

  • You can cancel Marlon Pro at any time by telling the person who sold you Marlon.
  • Pro stays on until the end of the month you've already paid for, then switches off. Part-months aren't refunded.
  • When Pro switches off, nothing is deleted: your invoices, returns and credit notes stay. Credit notes already given to stores still come off their next invoices, and regular Marlon keeps working.

Shelf photo

  • To read a shelf photo, Marlon sends it to Google's Gemini service. Marlon doesn't keep the photo after it's been read (the phone keeps a small copy in its own history).
  • Counts are made by AI and can be wrong. Check the shelf before relying on them.
  • Each company can check up to 100 shelf photos a day, and checks can be unavailable on some days if the service's free daily limit is reached.

Accountant summary

The accountant summary is put together from the invoices and credit notes in Marlon. It isn't tax or accounting advice. Your accountant still needs to check it before you file your taxes.

Questions about these terms? Contact the person who sold you Marlon.
