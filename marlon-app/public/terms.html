Marlon Pro Pro

Marlon Pro is an optional add-on to Marlon. It adds My Products, Shelf photo, Shelf Order, Returns and credit notes, and the Accountant summary and returns reports in the boss dashboard (or, for a solo business, in its own dashboard). Everything in regular Marlon keeps working exactly as before, with or without Pro.

Price

$25 (US dollars) per rep, per month

  • A solo business (one business on its own phone, without reps) counts as one rep.
  • Teams get the same automatic team discount as regular Marlon, by number of reps:
    1–4 repsNo discount
    5–9 reps10% off
    10–24 reps15% off
    25–49 reps20% off
    50–99 reps25% off
    100+ reps30% off
  • A special deal agreed with you is applied on top of the team discount (to the price after the team discount).
  • Your monthly Pro price, with any discount, is confirmed to you before Pro is switched on for your company.

Per-rep monthly billing

  • Pro is billed monthly, in advance, for every rep place your company has on Marlon: for a team, the number of rep places the company paid for (shown in the boss dashboard); for a solo business, one.
  • Rep places added during a month are billed from the start of the next month. Rep places removed stop being billed from the start of the next month.
  • Payment is made to the person who sold you Marlon, as agreed with them. Marlon doesn't take card payments inside the app.
  • If a monthly payment isn't made, Pro may be switched off until it is. Regular Marlon keeps working.
  • You'll be told at least 30 days before any change to the Pro price.

Cancellation

  • You can cancel Marlon Pro at any time by telling the person who sold you Marlon.
  • Pro stays on until the end of the month you've already paid for, then switches off. Part-months aren't refunded.
  • When Pro switches off, nothing is deleted: your invoices, returns, credit notes, orders and My Products stay. Credit notes already given to stores still come off their next invoices, and regular Marlon keeps working. Phones keep the last product list they had.

My Products

  • Your company can save its own products in My Products: name, size, price, how each is sold, and up to 3 photos per product taken with the phone camera. A company can save up to 200 products.
  • For a team, the boss adds, changes and deletes products in the boss dashboard, and every rep's phone gets the same list automatically. Reps can see the list but can't change it. A solo business manages its own list in its dashboard.
  • Once your company has saved products, the phones use that list for invoices, returns and orders. The products a phone had before are hidden, not deleted, and invoices already made don't change.
  • Your products and photos are kept on Marlon's server for your company only. No other company can see them. The person who sold you Marlon can see how many products your company has saved, but not the products or photos.
  • Adding or changing products needs an internet connection. Phones keep the last list so they work offline.

Shelf photo

  • To read a shelf photo, Marlon sends it to Google's Gemini service, together with your company's saved product names and sizes and one photo of each product (up to 40). Marlon doesn't keep the shelf photo after it's been read (the phone keeps a small copy in its own history).
  • Shelf photo looks only for your company's saved products. Anything else it sees is shown as "Unknown product".
  • Counts are made by AI and can be wrong. Check the shelf before relying on them.
  • Each rep can check up to 20 shelf photos a day. Checks can occasionally be unavailable if the photo service is down.

Shelf Order

  • After a shelf photo, a rep can make an order. Marlon suggests how many of each product to order (the target the rep sets, minus what's on the shelf). The rep can change any amount before saving, and is responsible for checking it.
  • The person placing the order for the store types their name and signs on the phone. The order is saved with their name, signature, the store, the date and the time, and is numbered (ORD-0001, ORD-0002…).
  • An order shows as Pending, Delivered or Cancelled. A delivered order can be turned into an invoice; the store still checks and signs the invoice as usual.
  • For a team, orders are sent to the boss dashboard when there's internet (they wait on the phone until then). The name and signature of the person who ordered are stored encrypted on Marlon's server, and the boss sees the signature only when they ask to. For a solo business, orders stay on its own phone.

Accountant summary

The accountant summary is put together from the invoices and credit notes in Marlon. It isn't tax or accounting advice. Your accountant still needs to check it before you file your taxes.

Marlon Pay

Marlon Pay is a separate app for stores. It replaces the paper book a store uses when it pays suppliers in cash on delivery: staff record each payment and the supplier signs for it on the store's device.

Price

  • Marlon Pay is a one-time payment, with no monthly plan. The price is confirmed to you in writing before you buy.
  • Payment is made to the person who sold you Marlon Pay, as agreed with them. Marlon Pay doesn't take card payments.

Links and devices

  • The store owner gets an owner link and gives each staff member their own staff link. Each link works only on the first device that opens it.
  • The owner can switch off any staff link, or give a staff place to someone new. The person who sold you Marlon Pay can switch the store off.

Signed records

  • Each payment is saved with the supplier, the date, the invoice number, the amount paid, the staff member, the time of signing and the supplier's signature.
  • Once the supplier has signed, the record is locked: nobody can change or delete it. The owner can add a dated correction note under it; the note can't be changed or removed either.
  • Marlon Pay doesn't give the supplier a receipt. The signed record is the store's own record that the payment was made.
  • Marlon Pay doesn't check that an invoice or an amount is correct. Staff and the supplier check them before signing.
  • When a supplier's rep uses Marlon, the store can open that invoice in Marlon Pay with the supplier, invoice number and total filled in. Paper invoices are typed in by hand.

Offline and your data

  • Records are saved on the device first, so Marlon Pay works without internet. They are sent to the store's account when there's a connection and stay on the device until then. If a device is lost or its browser data is cleared before its records were sent, those records can't be recovered.
  • Each store's suppliers, payments and signatures are private to that store. Signatures are stored encrypted (AES-256-GCM). The person who sold you Marlon Pay sees only counts (such as how many payments were signed) and problems (such as a second device trying to use a link), never supplier names, amounts or signatures.
